FAQs

A few answers before you shop.

Can I place a real order?

SmartShop is a student project. You can explore products and try the cart, but there is no checkout, payment, or delivery service.

How does the MEOW offer work?

Enter MEOW in the cart to take 10% off the product subtotal. Delivery and shipping stay the same. Applying the code again does not add another discount.

What happens to my demo balance?

You start with ৳5,000 and can add ৳1,000 at a time. The cart cannot exceed your balance. Refreshing the page starts a new session.

Are messages and subscriptions saved?

The forms demonstrate validation in your browser. Contact messages are not sent and newsletter email addresses are not saved or subscribed.
